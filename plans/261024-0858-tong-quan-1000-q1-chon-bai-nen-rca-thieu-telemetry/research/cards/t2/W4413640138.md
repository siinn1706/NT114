## Question
Does removing transient metric spikes and replacing BARO's max z-score with mean/median z-score make metric-based RCA more accurate (Sec. I, III)?

## Method (Sec. III, Alg. 1)
BARO = multivariate BOCD change-point + RobustScorer (median/IQR of pre-failure data). Additions: (a) outlier filter on post-fault segment (adaptive z-score, IQR, Tukey fences k=2.2, BOCD, DBSCAN, winsorization alpha=5); (b) aggregate z over the window by mean or median instead of max.

## Data and systems
RCAEval RE2 only: RE2-OB (12 services), RE2-SS (15), RE2-TT (64); 90 cases each, six fault types x 15 (Sec. III-C).

## Modalities
Metrics only (explicit "metric-centric", Sec. II).

## LLM
None.

## Baselines
Original BARO only (Table 5 max-z row; related-work table 2 compares by description). No other method run.

## Metrics
AC@1/3/5 and Avg@5 as in RCAEval; "fraction of cases with root cause in top-k" (Table 3 caption). Output is ranked metrics (mapped to services by RCAEval convention).

## Main results (Sec. IV)
- Abstract: Avg@5 0.77 -> 0.92; +0.21 s per case (Table 6: 0.53 s baseline, 0.64-0.74 s for IQR/Tukey/z/winsor, 2.45 s DBSCAN, 3.53 s BOCD; CPU t3a.large 8 GB).
- Table 5: max-z Avg@5 0.74 / 0.77 / 0.80 (OB/SS/TT); median-z 0.94 / 0.97 / 0.86; median unchanged by filters.
- Table 4: IQR/Tukey +0.18 OB, +0.20 SS, +0.03 TT over baseline.
- Sec. IV-A: MEM/DISK faults gain most (AC@1 to 1.00 on 30 inspected cases); DELAY/LOSS gain little because filtering also suppresses signal.

## Limitations / failure modes
Transient spikes may be genuine intermittent faults (packet-loss bursts, congestion) - future multi-stage filter (Sec. VI); small gains on DELAY/LOSS and on RE2-TT. Methods deterministic, so no error bars (Sec. IV intro).

## Missing telemetry
Not addressed: single modality (metrics); robustness is to spike noise, not to absent data. Not evidence for the missing-telemetry axis.

## Question
How to let cloud engineers investigate anomaly root causes faster by splitting work between a machine (data collection, clue recommendation) and a human (hypotheses, decisions) (Abstract; S1).

## Method
Four-stage workflow building/monitoring/reasoning/concluding (Fig. 1). Knowledge graph of entities and attributes built interactively (S4.1); users propose expand/refine hypotheses and a model collects clues ranked by BCPD change-point overlap relevance (S4.3, Eq. 1-2); results exported as a structured board (S4.4).

## Data and systems
Private data from one cloud provider: incident logs and KPI time series (S3.2). Two use cases: strategy inconsistency, normal-nodes error (S5.1, S5.2).

## Modalities
Numerical KPI time series and categorical event sequences / incident logs (S3.2).

## Limitations / failure modes

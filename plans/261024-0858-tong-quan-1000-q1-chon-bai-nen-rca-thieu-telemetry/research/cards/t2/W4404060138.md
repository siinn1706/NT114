## Question
Identify performance issues in multivariate metrics where correlation violations matter, with noisy training data, and point to the correlation-violated metrics (Abstract; S1).

## Method
Graph attention over metric relations plus GRU and multi-scale convolution temporal embedding (S3.3); Label-Conditional VAE with positive-unlabeled learning from 5% engineer-confirmed negatives (S3.4); attention weights localize violated metrics (S3.5).

## Data and systems
SMD (public, 28 machines) and two Huawei Cloud datasets with 21 and 31 services, 3-25 metrics, anomaly ratio 6.71%/5.88% (S4.1, Table 2). Localization labels exist only for the industrial sets.

## Modalities
Multivariate KPI metrics only.

## LLM
None.

## Baselines
18 detectors (Tables 3-4): OCSVM, IForest, LOF, DAGMM, LSTM, LSTM-VAE, OmniAnomaly, THOC, MTSAD, MSCRED, MTAD-GAT, GDN, GTA, TranAD, SLA-VAE, ACVAE, FuSAGNet, JumpStarter. Localization: DAGMM, LSTM-VAE, ISOLATE-Cor (S4.3.3).

## Metrics
Precision/recall/F1 with TP/FP/FN, point-adjusted segments, grid-searched threshold (S4.2.2); Hit@1/Hit@3 for localization (Table 6).

## Main results
- Table 4: F1 0.9346 (SMD), 0.9497 (A), 0.9514 (B), best of all.
- Table 6: Hit@1 0.8095/0.8387, Hit@3 0.9048/0.9354 on A/B, above baselines.
- Table 5 ablation: removing relational-temporal embedding or PU learning lowers F1; sensitivity of alpha, beta (S4.3.4).
- No repeated runs or significance tests.

## Artifact availability
github.com/WenweiGu/ISOLATE stated as released in S4.2.3; code and partial data (S5.3). Run guide and license not visible in text.

## Limitations / failure modes
No adaptation to evolving patterns (retrain weekly), cost grows with metric count, partial interpretability (S5.2); generalization beyond Huawei and SMD uncertain (S5.3).

## Missing telemetry
Not handled by modality; only training-data noise via PU learning, no controlled corruption or removal experiment.

## Question
Given an alarm KPI of a host cluster, rank the underlying VM KPIs that cause the degradation, efficiently and interpretably (Abstract; S3.1). Extends KPIRoot (ISSRE 2024).

## Method
Time-series decomposition (STL) plus U-Net autoencoder for anomaly segments (S3.3); Improved SAX representation for similarity (S3.4); Granger-causality F-test (S3.5); score = lambda x Jaccard similarity + (1-lambda) x causality (Algorithm 1). O(sqrt n) complexity (S3.6).

## Data and systems
Three private industrial datasets from CloudH availability zones: 16/6/7 host clusters, 21-803 VMs (S4.1.1, Table 1). Not microservices (S6.1).

## Modalities
KPI metrics only (VM KPIs, alarm KPI).

## LLM
None.

## Baselines
Detection (Table 2): 3-sigma, LOF, IF, Autoencoder, LSTM, KPIRoot. Localization (Table 3): Kendall, Spearman, CloudScout, LOUD, AID, HALO, CMMD (GNN), KPIRoot (S4.2.2).

## Metrics
F1 (with TP/FP/FN), Hit Rate@5/@10, NDCG@10 (S4.1.2).

## Main results
- Table 3: KPIRoot+ F1 0.884/0.891/0.871, Hit@10 0.934/0.967/0.936 on datasets A/B/C; best of all baselines; Abstract claims +2.9% to 35.7%.
- Table 4 ablation: removing Improved SAX or decomposition lowers NDCG@10 (e.g. 0.823 to 0.789 / 0.770 on A).
- S4.2.4: about 8 s per case vs more than 100 s for AID; Fig. 8 sensitivity on w and lambda.
- No repeated runs or significance tests.

## Artifact availability
Code: github.com/WenweiGu/KPIRoot (Data Availability statement); full data private, only desensitized samples public.

## Limitations / failure modes
SAX loses variation detail and threshold detection misses seasonal/point anomalies (S1, S2.3); Hit@5 bounded since root causes often exceed 5 (S4.2.2); manual label noise (S6.2); not applicable to invocation-graph microservice systems (S6.1).

## Missing telemetry
Not handled: single KPI modality, no experiment with absent or noisy KPIs; only hyperparameter sensitivity (S4.2.5).

# NT114 paper/code provenance audit

**Scope.** Read-only audit of the papers that underlie implemented code, benchmark/data lineage, related work, and the closest H1 prior. Evidence was checked against the current checkout and primary publication/repository pages on 2026-10-23.

## Repository evidence

- `nt114-rca/nt114-rca/src/rca/baro.py:1-7,19-32,41-42` explicitly says “BARO-style,” “a reimplementation, not RCAEval code,” pins the RCAEval commit, and documents the reproduced preprocessing/scoring steps. `baselines.py:18-23,104-112` wires it as an optional CPU baseline and maps ranked metrics to candidate services.
- `nt114-rca/nt114-rca/tests/test_baro.py:1-6,48-68` verifies fixture digests, rankings, services, scores, and the pinned clean commit; the fixture is external to the runtime module. `tests/fixtures/baro/provenance.json:1-26,240-246` records the official repository, isolated environment, 16 real DEV cases plus three deterministic edge cases, and the instrumentation method.
- The code lineage is the pinned official implementation files [`RCAEval/e2e/baro.py`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/phamquiluan/RCAEval/259ea4167160256a74ad30004aa3d96a998c846e/RCAEval/e2e/baro.py) and [`RCAEval/io/time_series.py`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/phamquiluan/RCAEval/259ea4167160256a74ad30004aa3d96a998c846e/RCAEval/io/time_series.py). The BARO paper itself describes an end-to-end anomaly-detection plus RCA system using multivariate Bayesian online change-point detection and robust statistical testing; the NT114 port starts after injection time is supplied.
- `docs/dataset_provenance.md:21-22` marks both `rcaeval-zenodo` and `rcaeval-hf` verified; `docs/dataset_discovery.md:55-59` identifies Zenodo 14590730 as canonical and the HF copy as derived. `src/rca/alignment.py:1-16` limits alignment to incident/telemetry identity; it explicitly requires separate checks for preprocessing, split, granularity, and candidate set.
- `nt114-decomprca/paper/paper.txt:1-33,52-90,420-433` identifies the DecompRCA manuscript, NeurIPS track, and the decomposition definition. `plans/reports/research-261019-tong-quan-elicit-chon-dong-gop-nt114.md:205-208,497-501,737-760,881-882` records the H1 boundary, external-reference rule, and missing-code caveat.
- `private/fulltext/261019/pace-lm-2309.05833.md:1-3,95-105,131-147` documents PACE-LM/LM-PACE's confidence design, 121,308 private incidents, GPT-4 pseudo-labels, ECE, and retrieval split. The local literature report records the same title/version discrepancy and H1 boundary at `plans/reports/research-261019-tong-quan-elicit-chon-dong-gop-nt114.md:201-208,497-508`.

## Decision and limits

1. Use **RCAEval** for benchmark/data/code lineage and **BARO-style** for the implemented non-LLM baseline.
2. Cite **DecompRCA** as related work and a novelty boundary; do not transfer its numbers without a full alignment receipt.
3. Cite **PACE-LM/LM-PACE** as the closest H1 prior; NT114's exact claim remains confidence/abstention at matched coverage under controlled telemetry removal on public fault-injection labels.
4. These four works establish identity, venue, and architectural role. They do **not** establish “three Q1 journal papers.” No Q1 claim is supported without an issuer, subject category, ranking year, journal identity, and paper-level evidence.

**Limitations.** The exact DecompRCA repository was unavailable at audit time; RCAEval 1.8.0 supplies the upstream metric implementation but is not evidence that NT114 implements DecompRCA. PACE-LM's private data and pseudo-labels limit direct reproducibility. This audit does not certify paper-level journal rankings.
